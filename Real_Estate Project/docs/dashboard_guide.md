# Dashboard Navigation Guide
## UAE Property Market Analysis — Power BI

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Executive Overview
**Purpose:** High-level market summary. Start here for a first-read of the UAE property market.

**Key visuals:**
- **4 KPI cards** — Total listings (492), Average price (AED 2.17M), Price range (AED 233K–14.5M), Dubai market share (62.4%)
- **Listings by City** — Clustered bar showing market concentration. Dubai's dominance is immediately visible.
- **Avg Price by Emirate** — Horizontal bar revealing RAK as the surprise price leader
- **Price Tier Donut** — How the 492 listings distribute across the five AED price bands
- **Top 3 Areas table** — Quick reference for premium area benchmarks
- **Bedroom Split** — Studio vs 3BR volume and pricing

**Slicer:** City filter (top right).

---

### Page 2 — RERA & Compliance Intelligence
**Purpose:** Regulatory analysis. Answers compliance gap questions for developers, investors, and regulators.

**Key visuals:**
- **5 KPI cards** — Compliance rate, RERA count, RERA avg price, non-RERA avg price, price premium
- **Compliance Rate by Emirate** — Horizontal bars from 100% (Dubai) to 0% (RAK, Ajman)
- **RERA vs Non-RERA Price** — Side-by-side column showing the 83% premium
- **RERA by Bedroom Type** — Studios (79.4%) vs 3BR (60.1%) registration rates
- **Price Gap Table** — RERA vs non-RERA pricing by bedroom category

**Slicers:** Year (top right)

---

### Page 3 — Area Intelligence & Investment Lens
**Purpose:** Neighbourhood-level analysis for investment decisions and development targeting.

**Key visuals:**
- **Scatter plot** — Each bubble = one area. X axis = listing volume, Y axis = average price. Quadrant labels identify Premium, Value, Emerging, and Budget zones.
- **Top 10 Premium Areas table** — Palm Jumeirah to Business Bay with city tagging
- **Best Value Areas bar** — Lowest price per bedroom, from Emirates City (AED 116K/BR) upward
- **Price per Bedroom by Emirate** — Column chart showing bedroom efficiency gap


**Slicer:** City and Year filter
